Nexo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Nexo ("Nexo", "we", "our", or "us") provides tools that allow users to connect supported social media accounts and manage communications through the Nexo service.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Nexo, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Nexo may process:

  • Basic Instagram account information and account identifiers.
  • Messages sent to or received by a connected Instagram professional account.
  • Message metadata, such as sender and recipient identifiers and timestamps.
  • Comments and other content where access has been explicitly authorized.
  • Authentication tokens required to maintain an authorized connection with Meta services.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed only as necessary to provide and operate Nexo, including to:

  • Connect an authorized Instagram account to Nexo.
  • Receive and manage Instagram communications.
  • Display and process messages and other authorized content.
  • Provide requested automation and account-management functionality.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Diagnose technical problems and prevent misuse.

3. Meta and Instagram Data

Nexo accesses Meta and Instagram data only through authorized Meta APIs and according to permissions granted by the user.

Nexo does not sell Instagram or Meta user data.

4. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate Nexo, such as hosting, database, and infrastructure providers, where necessary to provide the service.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

6. User Controls and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect their Instagram account from Nexo and may request deletion of personal information associated with the service.

Data deletion requests can be submitted using the contact information below.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nexo develops or its legal and technical requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
