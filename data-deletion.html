Nexo Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Nexo respects your privacy and allows users to request deletion of personal information associated with their use of the Nexo service.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your Nexo account or a connected Instagram account, please send an email to:

dundar.19074@gmail.com

Please use the subject line: Nexo Data Deletion Request.

Include enough information to identify the account associated with your request, such as the Instagram username connected to Nexo. Do not send your Instagram password or other sensitive login credentials.

What Happens After a Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, Nexo will identify and delete personal information associated with the requesting user that Nexo is required or permitted to delete.

Some information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Disconnecting Instagram

Users may also revoke Nexo's access to their Instagram or Meta account through the relevant Meta account settings. Revoking access prevents Nexo from accessing new information through the revoked authorization.
